Career Fair Registration & Payment

Where do employers register for the Career Fair?

Employer registration and payment information should be completed through the Engineering Career Resource Center. Visit the ECRC website →

Who should I contact about registration or payment?

Please contact the Engineering Career Resource Center at eng-careerfair@umich.edu.

Where can I find cancellation or refund information?

Cancellation, refund, and payment policies should be referenced through ECRC’s Employer Registration Guide. View ECRC information →

Receptions & SWE/TBP Events

What are Receptions?

Receptions are relaxed pre-fair networking events hosted before the Career Fair. They help companies connect with students in a more casual, conversational setting.

Which reception night should my company attend?

Reception nights correspond to Career Fair attendance days. Companies attending the Monday fair attend the Sunday reception, and companies attending the Tuesday fair attend the Monday reception. View reception details →

Can companies participate in other SWE/TBP events?

Yes. Employers may have opportunities to participate in receptions, sponsorships, and other SWE/TBP-related events connected to the Career Fair.

SWE-related events: swe.car.directors@umich.edu

TBP-related events: tbp-corporate@umich.edu
